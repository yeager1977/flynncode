Local GUI fallback after the browser pane. Drives the in-app preview pane only, via the reserved app id `flynncode:browser-pane` (exact match); screenshots come from the preview pane view itself. Disabled by default; requires per-app allowlist plus per-action approval (never self-approving). Observe actions (screenshot) and mutate actions (click/type/keypress/scroll) can each be tightened off in config (computer_use.tiers.observe / .mutate.enabled); a disabled tier fails before approval is even requested. An unconfirmed outcome means the action may or may not have run: never claim either, re-verify with a screenshot or ask the user. Never background operation.
