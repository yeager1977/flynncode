Schedules a prompt back into this session: recurring (the default; expires 7 days after creation) or once at the next match (recurring false). cron is 5 fields (minute hour day-of-month month day-of-week) in server local time, e.g. "*/5 * * * *". The prompt is delivered when the session is idle, so write it to stand on its own; a prompt that starts with /command runs that command. At most 20 jobs per session. Delete a job with cron_delete once it is no longer needed.
