Send a message to another agent's session.

Use this to coordinate with a session you or the user knows about (for example a
subagent session you started with the task tool, identified by its task_id), to
provide updates or ask for coordination without waiting for that session to
finish its current work.

Notes:
1. The target session must exist. A busy session is steered at the next safe
   turn boundary. An idle session receives the message as a queued turn.
2. The message is delivered as a synthetic peer message. The target agent's
   next assistant text is sent back to your session as a peer reply.
3. You are not blocked. Do not wait in this turn for the reply.
